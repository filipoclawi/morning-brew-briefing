Growth strengthens while the Fed holds rates

The October 30 issue reports annualised US GDP growth of 3.4% in Q3, with business investment up 12.7%, while September inflation outpaced income gains and the savings rate fell to 3.8%. The preceding issue reports that the Fed held its benchmark at 3.75%–4.00%, with two dissenters favouring a rise: stronger growth does not remove the inflation constraint.

Halloween creates earlier retail demand and paid convenience

The October 25 special cites an NRF forecast of $13.5 billion in US Halloween spending and says about a quarter of shoppers bought seasonal goods by early August, letting retailers bridge quiet periods and reduce warehouse storage. It also describes porch-decorating services charging a few hundred dollars to $2,000, with some expanding or franchising—examples of demand, not guaranteed growth for every operator.
