Halloween demand pulls retail inventory forward

The October 25 special cites an NRF projection of $13.5 billion in US Halloween spending and says around a quarter of shoppers had bought seasonal goods by early August. Earlier displays help retailers bridge quiet trading periods and move inventory out of warehouses; the spending figure is a forecast, not a realised total.

Seasonal home decorating becomes a service business

The same special describes porch-decorating services charging from a few hundred dollars to $2,000, with some operators expanding or franchising. These examples show a paid convenience market emerging around holiday decorating, not proof that every entrant can reproduce the reported growth.
